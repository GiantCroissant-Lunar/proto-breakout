# Endless Mode

> Status: Future

Infinite brick waves. Survival scoring.

## Concept

- Bricks spawn in waves from the top
- Wave difficulty increases over time
- Score = total bricks destroyed before losing all lives
- No "win" condition — pure high score chase

## Mechanics

- New rows slide in from top every N seconds
- Ball speed gradually increases
- Power-ups spawn more frequently to compensate
- Difficulty curve: more rows, faster ball, tougher bricks

## Scoring

- Base points per brick × combo multiplier
- Combo: consecutive hits without paddle touch
- High score saved to localStorage
