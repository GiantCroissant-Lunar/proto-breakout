# Time Attack

> Status: Future

Clear levels as fast as possible. Timer-based scoring.

## Concept

- Pre-designed level layouts
- Timer counts up from 0:00
- Goal: clear all bricks in minimum time
- Bonus points for time remaining

## Mechanics

- Fixed levels (not random grids)
- Some bricks take multiple hits
- Timer penalty for losing a life (+5 seconds)
- Star rating: ⭐⭐⭐ based on completion time

## Levels

Each level has a unique brick pattern:
1. Standard grid
2. Diamond shape
3. Fortress (wall around special brick)
4. Spiral pattern
5. Checkerboard of multi-hit bricks
