# Arkanoid (1986) - Taito's Evolution of Breakout

**Release Date:** July 1986 (Japan)  
**Developer:** Taito Corporation  
**Designers:** Akira Fujita, Hiroshi Tsujino  
**Composer:** Hisayoshi Ogura (Zuntata)  
**Platform:** Arcade (Taito Classic hardware)  

---

## Historical Context

Arkanoid was created during an **internal competition** at Taito where two teams of designers had to create block breaker games. The futuristic neon aesthetic was inspired by the 1982 film **Tron**.

**Commercial Success:**
- Highest-grossing table arcade cabinet of **1987 in Japan**
- Highest-grossing conversion kit of **1987 in the United States**
- Top-grossing arcade game for **6 consecutive months** in Japan (Aug 1986 - Feb 1987)

---

## Story

The starship **Arkanoid** is attacked by a mysterious entity from space named **DOH**. A small paddle-shaped craft, the **Vaus**, is ejected from the Arkanoid but becomes trapped in distorted space. The player must clear 33 stages to confront DOH.

**Final Boss:** Stage 33 features DOH, a large Moai statue-like head (in most versions; NES has 36 stages)

---

## Gameplay Innovations Over Breakout

Arkanoid transformed the simple Breakout formula by adding:

1. **Power-up capsules** dropped from certain bricks
2. **Multiple brick types** with different properties
3. **Enemy sprites** that interfere with the ball
4. **33 unique stage layouts** (vs. Breakout's 2)
5. **Boss battle** on final stage
6. **Continue system** (except on final stage)

---

## Controls

| Control | Function |
|---------|----------|
| Paddle (rotary) | Move Vaus left/right |
| Fire Button | Fire lasers (when equipped) OR release caught ball |
| 1-2 Players | Start game |

### Paddle Physics
Where the ball hits the Vaus determines bounce angle:
- **Silver center:** Sharp angle bounce
- **Red bands (near sides):** 45° angle bounce
- **Very edges:** Shallow angle bounce

---

## Stage Structure

- **33 total stages** (36 on NES)
- **Extra lives** awarded at:
  - 20,000 points
  - 60,000 points
  - Every 60,000 points thereafter
- **Continue allowed** after game over (except on Stage 33)
- **Stage 33:** Boss fight against DOH (no continues possible)

### Stage Layouts
Each of the 32 normal stages has a unique brick arrangement. Stage images available at StrategyWiki:

## Legacy

Arkanoid spawned a major franchise:
- **Tournament Arkanoid** (1987) - US-exclusive with new levels
- **Arkanoid: Revenge of Doh** (1987) - True sequel with new mechanics
- **Arkanoid: Doh It Again** (1997)
- **Arkanoid Returns** (1997)
- **Arkanoid DS** (2007)
- **Arkanoid Live!** (2009, Xbox Live Arcade)
- **Arkanoid Plus!** (2009, WiiWare)
- **Arkanoid vs. Space Invaders** (2017, mobile)
- **Arkanoid: Eternal Battle** (2022, modern remake)

**Cultural Impact:**
- First game to enter **Popular Computing Weekly Hall of Fame** (1987)
- **Touhou Project** (1996) was heavily influenced by Arkanoid gameplay
- Appears in **Life Is Strange: True Colors**

---

## Ports

Arkanoid was ported to virtually every home computer and console of the era:
- NES (with custom paddle controller)
- Commodore 64, Amiga, Atari ST
- ZX Spectrum, Amstrad CPC, BBC Micro
- Apple II, Apple IIGS, MSX
- DOS, PC-9801
- Mobile (iOS, 2009)
