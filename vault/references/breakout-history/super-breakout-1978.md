# Super Breakout (1978) - Atari Sequel

**Release Date:** 1978  
**Developer:** Atari, Inc.  
**Platform:** Arcade (Motorola M6502 @ 375 KHz)  
**Series:** Breakout  

---

## Overview

Super Breakout is the official sequel to Breakout, released two years after the original. It introduced **three distinct game modes**, each with unique mechanics and brick layouts.

---

## Game Modes

### 1. Double Breakout
- **52 orange blocks** (5-14 points each)
- **52 green blocks** (1-6 points each)
- **Two paddles** (one for each player in 2-player mode)
- **Two balls** in play simultaneously

**Layout:** Two separate brick walls, one for each player/ball

### 2. Cavity Breakout
- **44 orange blocks** (7-21 points each)
- **52 green blocks** (1-9 points each)
- **One paddle**
- **Three balls** (2nd and 3rd must be **freed** before they come into play)

**Special Mechanic:** Balls are trapped inside cavities and must be released by hitting specific blocks

### 3. Progressive Breakout ⭐ (Most Innovative)
- **52 blue blocks** (7 points each)
- **52 green blocks** (5 points each)
- **One paddle**
- **One ball**

**Unique Mechanics:**
- Blocks **lower toward the paddle** at a rate determined by how many times the ball lands on the paddle
- As blocks are destroyed, **new rows appear at the top** and descend
- Descend rate **increases over time**
- **Infinite scoring possible** (score rolls over at 10,000)

This mode essentially invented the "descending brick" mechanic later seen in games like Puzzle Bobble/Bust-A-Move and modern mobile breakers.

## Scoring Comparison

| Mode | Block Types | Point Range | Special Feature |
|------|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Double | Orange, Green | 1-14 | Two balls, two paddles |
| Cavity | Orange, Green | 1-21 | Trapped balls to free |
| Progressive | Blue, Green | 5, 7 | Descending blocks, infinite play |

---

## Technical Innovations

Super Breakout was one of the first arcade games to offer **multiple game modes** selectable at the cabinet, giving players variety and replay value. The Progressive mode's descending brick mechanic was particularly innovative and influenced puzzle game design for decades.

---

## Legacy

Super Breakout established the template for:
- **Multiple game modes** as a standard feature
- **Progressive difficulty** (blocks descending faster)
- **Special brick behaviors** (cavity-enclosed balls)
- Later games: **Breakout 2000** (Atari Jaguar, 1996)
